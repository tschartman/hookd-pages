Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2, 2026

HOOKD ("we," "our," or "the app") is a music discovery application. We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how we handle your information.

Information We Collect

HOOKD collects only the minimum information necessary to provide the service:

  • Streaming service connections. When you connect Spotify, Apple Music, or YouTube Music, we receive an authentication token that allows us to create playlists on your behalf. We do not access your listening history, library, or personal data from these services beyond what is needed for playlist export.
  • Discovery activity. Your saved tracks, collections, and mission history are stored to provide your personal discovery library. This data is associated with your account and is not shared with third parties.
  • Basic analytics. We may collect anonymous usage data (such as which features are used most) to improve the app. This data cannot be used to identify you.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or phone number unless you provide it directly.
  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not access your contacts, camera, or microphone.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising purposes.

Third-Party Services

HOOKD integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Spotify — for authentication and playlist export. Governed by Spotify's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple Music — for authentication and playlist export. Governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube Music — for authentication and playlist export. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase — for backend data storage. Governed by Supabase's Privacy Policy.

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, which provides encrypted storage and secure authentication. Authentication tokens for streaming services are stored locally on your device using encrypted secure storage and are never transmitted to our servers.

Data Retention & Deletion

You can disconnect any streaming service at any time from the Library tab, which removes the associated authentication token from your device. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, contact us and we will process your request within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

HOOKD is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or your data, contact us at privacy@hookdmusic.com.
